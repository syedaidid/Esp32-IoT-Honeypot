**Service:** IoT Honeypot Network Security Monitoring
**Provider:** [YOUR NAME / COMPANY]
**Client:** ____________________________________
**Date:** ______________

## 1. What we deliver
1.1 One ESP32 monitoring device installed at the client's premises, connected to the client's
2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
1.2 A cloud monitoring console (web dashboard) with: live attack feed, world threat map,
attacker profiles, automatic detection alerts, and CSV/PDF forensic report export.
1.3 Optional second sensor on a cloud server to monitor attacks against the client's public
internet address, if selected in the scope (Section 4).

## 2. What we do NOT deliver
2.1 The device is a **detection and evidence tool, not a firewall**. It does not block or prevent
attacks on the client's real devices, network, or systems.
2.2 The service does not alter the client's router, firewall, or security settings, and requires
**no port forwarding**.

## 3. Client obligations
3.1 Provide: Wi-Fi access (2.4 GHz SSID + password), power outlet within ~10 m of the router,
and a named contact for alerts.
3.2 Keep router firmware updated and maintain existing antivirus/firewall.
3.3 Notify Provider of any network changes (new router, ISP change, moved premises).

4.2 Monthly service (dashboard + reports + hosting): RM ______
4.3 Monitoring scope (tick): □ LAN only  □ Internet-facing + LAN  □ Both

## 5. Data & privacy
5.1 The service records real attackers' IP addresses, credentials attempted, and timestamps.
5.2 Public reports are de-identified. Raw data is disclosed only to the Client, and to law
enforcement if legally compelled.
5.3 Client consents to storage of captured data on Provider's cloud server for [30] days, after
which it is deleted unless a report is exported.

## 6. Liability (read carefully)
6.1 The service is provided "as is" on a best-effort basis. **Provider is not liable for any loss,
damage, or breach suffered by the Client** arising from attacks on the Client's real devices or
network, even if the service was active at the time.
6.2 Provider's total liability is limited to the service fees paid in the previous 3 months.

## 7. Term & termination
7.1 Month-to-month; either party may cancel with [14] days written notice.
7.2 On termination, the device is returned within [7] days and the Client's data is deleted.

map, and report export) and accepts the device on: ______________
8.2 Hardware warranty: [30] days for manufacturer defects; not valid for tampering, water
damage, or power surges.
